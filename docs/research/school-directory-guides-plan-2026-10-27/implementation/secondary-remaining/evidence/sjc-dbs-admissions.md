## Application for Secondary 1 Discretionary Places 2027/2028

I. Number of Discretionary Places: 54

II. Application

1. Distribution of Application Form

- Students from St. Joseph’s Primary School Please follow instructions given by St. Joseph’s Primary School.
- Students from other primary schools Starting from 23 November 2026, applicants may download the application form from our school website (DOWNLOAD) or obtain it in person from the School Office.
2. Submission of Application Form Please submit your application in person to the School Office. Applications sent by post, fax, or email will not be accepted. For those who opt for submission through the SSPA e-Platform, please refer to EDB guidelines. Application Period : 4 – 18 January 2027 (except Sundays and Public Holidays) Office Hours : Monday to Friday : 9:00 am – 4:30 pm Saturday : 9:00 am – 12:00 noon Documents Required : i. Photocopy of the applicant’s Hong Kong Identity Card* ii. The Education Bureau’s Application Form iii. The School’s Application Form iv. Photocopies of supporting documents, including school report cards for Primary 5 (whole year) and Primary 6 (first term) Please note that letters of recommendation from primary school teachers or the principal are NOT required. Late applications will NOT be accepted. *Non-local applicants should extra enclose the following in applications: a) A copy of the approval from the Director of Immigration for the purpose of stay in Hong Kong. b) A copy of a valid travel document showing the permitted duration of stay in Hong Kong.

III. Interview All applicants will be invited for an interview on 27 February 2027 (Saturday).

IV. Admission Criteria and Weightin gs 收生準則及比重

1. Excellent/good conduct is essential for selection. 優秀或良好的操行不可或缺。
2. The overall admission criteria and weightings will be as follows: 收生準則及比重如下：

| Admission Criteria 收生準則 | Weightings 比重 |
| --- | --- |
| a) Performance in primary school (report cards as reference) as revealed in: 小學期間的表現（以學校成績表為參考）： – Academic and EDB’s rank order (25%) 學業成績及教育局提供的學生成績次第(25%) – Extra-curricular activities and awards (15%) 課外活動及獎項(15%) – Conduct (10%) 操行(10%) | 50% |
| b) Performance in the interview 面試表現 | 50% |

 IV. Release of Results Parents of all successful applicants will be notified of their results for Discretionary Places on 31 March 2027 (Wednesday) by email and telephone. The allocation results will be released on 6 July 2027 (Tuesday) through Central Allocation.

## G 7 (F.1) Admissions:

Distribution of G7 (F.1) application forms: 17 October–17 November 2026 Deadline for the return of G7 application forms: 17 November 2026 (Tuesday)

 Application forms and relevant information can be obtained:

i. via the school website
ii. by mail (please post a self-addressed stamped [$5.6] A4-sized envelope to the School)

 Applicants will not be asked to fill in any information on family income on the application form.

Admissions Talk for Parents of DPS & Other Invited Schools: 09:30–10:30, 17 October 2026 (Saturday) Admissions Talk for the Public: 11:30–12:30, 17 October 2026 (Saturday) Both sessions will be conducted in Cantonese. Tickets to the Admissions Talk will be sent to selected primary schools. Parents may contact your son’s current school for tickets. Parents of other schools may obtain tickets to the second session by mailing a self-addressed stamped envelope to the school on or before 9 October 2026. Kindly indicate “Grade 7 (F.1) Admissions Talk Tickets” on the envelope. Note that tickets are distributed on a “first-come-first-served” basis. The G7 Admission Talks will be recorded and uploaded to the school website.

Annual school fees Secondary 1 - 6 (NSS, G7 - G12) $65,980 International Baccalaureate (G10 Bridging Course) $65,980 International Baccalaureate (G11) $151,180 International Baccalaureate (G12) $151,180 School fees will be paid in ten installments from G7 to G11 in both NSS and IB. For G12 in NSS and IB, the installments will be seven and eight respectively. The school fees may be revised, subject to EDB’s approval.

Interviews Interviews will be conducted in both Cantonese and English. Candidates may be asked to participate in music auditions and sports trials.

| Criteria for the selection of Grade 7 (Form 1) boys | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Academic results | 60% |
| Extracurricular achievements | 20% |
| Performance in the interviews | 15% |
| Others (e.g. father, brother are old boys; brother studying in DBS) | 5% |

 Release of results Results will be released in late March or early April 2027. Parents of successful applicants will be asked to sign a Letter of Undertaking (Enrollment Letter) from late March to early April 2027 declaring that their son will not participate in the Secondary School Places Allocation.

 Admission and Selection DBS will not participate in the Secondary School Places Allocation. All Grade 7 (Form 1) students will be admitted through the School’s own admission and selection process. Our selection process is based on a fair and transparent principle. Parents are advised not to obtain a great number of reference letters, including those from coaches and instructors, at an excessive cost.
