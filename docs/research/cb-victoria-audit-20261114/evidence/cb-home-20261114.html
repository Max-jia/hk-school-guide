Regarding the admission Process for AY 2027-28

14 Sep 2026

The 2027-28 Pre-Nursery Class(PN) is open for enrollment now.  Please visit the admission site here for PN application.

Due to PN students being directly promoted to K1, there are a limited number of external places available for K1.Therefore, our school will not be accepting enrollment for the AY 2027-28 Nursery Class (K1).

If you wish to apply for your child to be considered for K1, please complete the e-Preliminary Record. We will keep it on file and contact you if places are available.
